- **$0:** a swinging price tag reads "DUE TODAY $0", then flips to "PAY WHEN YOU LOVE IT".

- **Homeowner:** a phone searches, opens the site and calls.
- **No website vs. next guy:** two phones side by side. "No website" shows an error; "Gets the call" shows a ringing call.
- **Build / Review / Love it:** stamped tiles, then a NewWay receipt prints with "ONE-TIME" payment and "Monthly fees $0.00" highlighted.
- **CTA:** the logo, a chat bubble, and a neo-brutalist button pressed by a hand; ends on "Right now!".

**Captions:** placed in the lower-middle of the frame (y=1300), word-synced, with quiet typing.
