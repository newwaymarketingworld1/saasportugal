# Reels "Contractors" (Drew voiceover): source

This is a product-style ad: a premium 3D browser window shows the real client sites, and an iPhone tells the homeowner's journey.

**Scenes:**
- **Hook:** "CONTRACTORS." with a 3D fan of contractor sites (JGC, Deck & Fence, Brothers Assis).
- **Build first, $0:** a wireframe builds into the Alpha Team site, with a "Built first" chip, a "Due today $0.00" card and a "Pay when you love it" pill.
- **Trades:** the window switches site on each spoken trade, with a matching tag highlighting below:
  - roofers: Alpha Vertical roofs
  - remodelers: remodeling site
  - painters: Alpha Vertical facades
  - general contractors: JGC, then a 3D fan of sites
- **"Homeowners check your website before they call":** the phone searches "roofer near me", opens the mobile site (real JGC photo) and taps "Call now", which opens the calling screen.
- **"No website? They call the next guy":** "No website" in the results, then a "This site can't be reached" error, then the call goes to "Next Guy Roofing".
- **Build, review, love it, pay once, no monthly fees:** the Brothers Assis site with review comments, a heart burst, a "One-time · Paid" card and a "Subscription" line struck out at $0/mo.
- **CTA:** a NewWay chat on the phone and the "Send us a message" button being tapped, then "Right now!" and the logo lockup.

**Captions:** word-synced at the top of the frame. The typing sound is about 8 dB quieter than in previous videos.

**Music:** an original upbeat groove (C-G-Am-F, 116 bpm) with a tension break on "No website? They call the next guy".
