# Reels "Silence" (Kristen voiceover, v2): source

**Concept:** the "silent loss" of customers. The first half is suspense, with heartbeat, drone and near silence on "Nobody tells you". At the NewWay reveal the "N" from the logo fills the screen and the beat comes in.

**Captions:** the captions are typed letter by letter in sync with every spoken word.
- Word timings come from `words.json`, extracted with `words.py` using sherpa-onnx zipformer token timestamps.
- Each letter gets a mechanical keystroke, and there is a yellow cursor.
- Yellow words mark emphasis. The yellow marker highlights key phrases, and "AI" gets a strike-through.
- The caption engine and the scenes are in `main.js`.
- The font is Montserrat. Azonix could not be downloaded in this environment; if the .otf is uploaded to the Drive, it can be added.

**Drive assets used:**
- 12 client logos (`lg/`) at "$20 million in ads".
- Deck & Fence as the sponsored ad.
- An Alpha Team scan at "we fix the part you can't see".
- A carousel of 8 projects (`c/` plus the v1/v2 videos).
- The Alpha Vertical video for build → review → change.
- Pedro & Gustavo at "Built by real people".
- The logo: on yellow, white in the CTA, and the "N" as a transition.
