# NewWay: brand motion video (English, no narration)

This is a 39.5s brand presentation with on-screen captions only. The captions are typed letter by letter, with one key sound per letter. The audio is sound effects plus a very subtle ambient bed; there is no voice or music.

1. The NewWay "N" is drawn as one continuous line, then filled. This follows the brand concept: the N in continuous movement, "a new way".
2. "Every business deserves to be seen."
3. "But most websites look the same." Identical templates fill the screen, then one real site stands out.
4. Logo reveal: yellow symbol and white wordmark. Caption: "A new way forward."
5. "Websites that convert." Portfolio carousel.
6. "Ads that scale." $20M+ counter with the client logos.
7. "100+ websites delivered across the U.S." Site grid.
8. "Built by real people. Not AI. Not templates." Pedro & Gustavo.
9. "How it works": 01 build first, 02 review & change, 03 pay only when you love it.
10. "One payment. No monthly fees." The 12 months are struck through.
11. Outro on yellow: black logo, "Send us a message.", "Websites · Ads · Growth".

Only clean transitions are used: blur zoom, iris, motion-blurred push, and the NewWay "N" sting.
