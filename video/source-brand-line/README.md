# NewWay: brand motion "The Line" (English, no narration, no typing sound)

**Concept:** the brand idea is the N in continuous movement, "a new way". One yellow line leads the whole video, and the camera follows it across a single long canvas (dot-grid background with parallax outline type). There are no cuts:
1. The line draws the N, which becomes the real symbol, followed by the wordmark.
2. It draws a browser frame. A website builds inside, a cursor clicks, the real site is revealed, then it flips through Alpha Team, Brothers Assis, JGC and Poenza. Floating badges appear around it.
3. It becomes the ads growth chart (Clicks / Leads / Sales), with the $20M+ counter and 12 client logos orbiting in 3D.
4. It draws a serpentine grid filled with 8 projects. Counter: 100+ websites across the U.S.
5. It loops around Pedro and Gustavo, then strikes through "Not AI." and "Not templates."
6. It becomes the process path: build first, review & change, pay when you love it.
7. The camera zooms out to reveal the whole journey drawn by the line.
8. Lockup: the line draws the N again, then the logo and "A new way forward."

**Sound:** effects only: the line-drawing sound, whooshes on the camera moves, pops, flips, impacts, shimmer and a subtle ambient bed. There is no typing.
