# Reels "Searching": source

**Concept:** someone is searching for your business right now, and in 3 seconds they will choose the competitor because of the website. The video then turns into the NewWay offer.

**Captions:** typed letter by letter, synced to each of the 133 spoken words.
- The caption engine is in `main.js`.
- Word timings come from `words.py`, using sherpa-onnx zipformer timestamps.
- Each letter gets a keystroke sound, and there is a yellow cursor.
- Yellow words and the marker highlight emphasis. "AI" and "TEMPLATE" get a strike-through.

**Transitions:** clean only. Motion-blurred pushes, blur zoom, dip to black, crossfade, a circular iris into yellow scenes, and a NewWay sting (colour dissolve, then the N in the centre). The yellow and white bar wipes were removed.

**Drive assets used:**
- 12 client logos at $20M.
- JGC as the "competitor".
- The Alpha Vertical video under "we build first".
- Brothers Assis in the review.
- A 6-site grid at "real designers".
- An 8-project carousel at "100+ websites".
- Pedro & Gustavo.
- The logo (on yellow, white in the CTA, and the N in the transitions).
